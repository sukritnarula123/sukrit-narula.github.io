About

Background

I am a Cardiology Fellow at Yale New Haven Health, training through the ABIM Physician-Scientist Research Pathway. I earned my medical degree from the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, where I graduated with a Distinction in Research, and completed my Internal Medicine residency at Yale New Haven Health alongside a PhD in Health Research Methods at McMaster University.

Prior to Yale, I conducted research in cardiovascular disease epidemiology as a fellow at the Population Health Research Institute in Hamilton, Canada, with a focus on epidemiologic modeling and implementation where I worked on identifying markers of cardiovascular and cardiometabolic disease and evaluating how they can be translated into clinical use. I retain a special interest in the evaluation of novel markers of cardiovascular disease.

I am the author of multiple peer-reviewed manuscripts, including first-author publications in The Lancet and the Journal of the American College of Cardiology. I currently serve as Associate Commissioning Editor at Heart (the official journal of the British Cardiovascular Society) and as a peer reviewer for JACC (and its sister journals), Nature Communications, and The Lancet, among others. I am Board Certified by the American Board of Internal Medicine.

Research Focus

Three threads

01

Epidemiologic Modeling & Implementation

Identifying markers of cardiovascular and cardiometabolic disease at population scale, and studying how they move from discovery into clinical practice.

02

Molecular Epidemiology

A continued interest in the plasma proteome and genetic architecture of disease — including Mendelian randomization approaches to causal inference.

03

Cardiovascular Imaging

Earlier work on computational phenotyping of echocardiographic imaging, and ongoing interest in applications of echocardiography.
